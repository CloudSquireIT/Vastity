Privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Vastity has no accounts, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

What stays on your device

Your location, settings, observation log, badges and mission progress are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data removes them.

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com)An approximate position, rounded to about 10 kmCloud and weather forecasts for “Tonight”
CelesTrak and tle.ivanstanojevic.meNo data about youOrbital data for the International Space Station
Vastity website (GitHub Pages)No data about youCatalogue updates and high-resolution textures

Like any request on the internet, these services see the IP address the request comes from. Vastity sends no identifiers, names or other data.

Permissions

  • Location: to work out the sky you see. You can also choose a place manually.
  • Motion sensors: to show the part of the sky your phone is pointing at.
  • Camera: only for augmented reality. Images are never saved or sent.
  • Notifications: reminders for eclipses and ISS passes, scheduled on the device.

Children

Vastity is suitable for all ages and collects no one's data, children included.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository or write to the address shown on the store page.

Confidentialité

Vastity n'a pas de compte, n'affiche pas de publicité et n'utilise aucun outil d'analyse ou de pistage. Nous ne vendons ni ne partageons de données personnelles, car nous n'en collectons pas.

Votre position, vos réglages, votre journal d'observations, vos badges et vos missions sont enregistrés uniquement sur votre appareil. Ils disparaissent si vous désinstallez l'app ou effacez les données du navigateur.

Autorisations

  • Position : pour calculer le ciel que vous voyez. Vous pouvez aussi choisir un lieu à la main.
